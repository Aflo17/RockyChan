Need help with RockyChan? Email aflores@dreamsflo.com and we'll get back to you within two business days.

Common questions

How do I invite my family?
Open Family, tap Share link, and send it to them. They can also enter the code when they set up the app.

How do I add another pet?
Go to Settings → Add another pet.

How do I make a new Chan?
Tap your pet's face at the top of Home, then tap Make a new Chan. Each pet gets 3 new Chans a month.

I'm not getting notifications.

  • In RockyChan, check Settings → Notifications.
  • Then check that notifications are allowed for RockyChan in your phone's Settings.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete account. This can't be undone.

Is RockyChan a vet?
No. RockyChan keeps track of care for your family and your vet. It can't tell what something is. Call your vet about anything new or worrying.
