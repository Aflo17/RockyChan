RockyChan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

RockyChan is a pet-care app made by DreamsFlo LLC ("we", "us"). It helps a family care for their pets together. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your account
    • You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive a user ID, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address) and, if you allow it, your name.
    • You can change your name in the app.
  • Your family
    • The family name, who is in it, and each person's role (owner or caregiver).
    • Invite codes you create.
  • Your pets
    • Name, dog or cat, an optional gender, and the pet's Chan picture.
    • If you add them: a birthday or an age guess, breed, weights, and your vet's contact details.
  • Care logs
    • Feeding, bathroom and medicine entries.
    • Who logged each entry and when.
    • Details you add, such as "pee" or "poop".
  • Medicines
    • Names, doses, strengths and schedules that you enter.
  • Body map
    • Spots you mark on your pet: the kind, rough size, notes, dates and any photos you add.
  • Vet records
    • Photos and PDF files you upload, with the names and dates you give them.
  • Language
    • The language you pick in Settings, and your phone's language, so the app and notifications use it.
  • Purchases
    • If you buy the family plan, Apple handles the payment. We receive the plan, its status and dates from Apple through RevenueCat, linked to your account.
    • We never see your card details.
  • Notification settings
    • Which alerts you want.
    • A push token so your phone can receive them.
  • Error reports
    • When something goes wrong, the app records what failed, on which screen, the app version and your phone's system version, so we can fix it.
    • These reports don't include your photos or files.

Pet photos and the Chan

When you make a Chan, the app first asks your permission. It then sends a smaller copy of your pet's photo to OpenAI, the service that checks the photo shows a dog or cat and draws the Chan.

  • What we keep: we don't keep the photo you send for this. We keep only the finished Chan picture.
  • What OpenAI keeps: OpenAI processes the photo under its API terms and may keep it for a limited time to prevent abuse. OpenAI does not use it to train its models.

How we use it

  • To run the app: showing your family's pets, logs, medicines, body map and vet records to the people in your family.
  • To send the notifications you turned on: care alerts and medicine reminders.
  • To keep the app working and fix problems.
  • To tell the RockyChan team about new sign-ups, new families and purchases: internal emails with your name, email, family name and pets' names. We don't send you marketing emails.

We don't sell your information. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't show ads.

Who can see it

  • Your family: only people in your family can see your family's pets, logs, medicines, body map, photos and vet records.
  • Sharing with a vet: when you share records, the app uses your phone's share options. You choose who receives them.
  • Service providers: we use a few providers to run the app.
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in, hosted in the United States.
    • OpenAI: Chan drawing.
    • Apple and Google: sign-in and push notifications.
    • Expo: app delivery and notifications.
    • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions with Apple.
    • Resend: sends the internal emails to the RockyChan team.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Deleting your account: go to Settings → Delete account.
    • Families where you are the only member are deleted, along with their pets, logs, photos and files.
    • In shared families, your past logs stay, without your name, so the rest of the family keeps its history.
  • Leaving a family: in Family, choose Leave family.
  • On your phone: the app keeps a copy of your family's information on your phone so it works offline. It's removed when you sign out.
  • Your family plan: deleting your account doesn't cancel a family plan; cancel it in your App Store settings. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as the law requires.
  • Getting help: if you can't use the app, email aflores@dreamsflo.com and we'll help.

Children

RockyChan isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
